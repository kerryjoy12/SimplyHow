This page is our promise about how content gets made here. It applies to every article on SimplyHow, without exception.

## 1. Independence

Our editorial decisions are made by our editors and nobody else. Advertising relationships, affiliate programs, public relations pitches and free product samples never influence whether a guide is written, what it recommends, or how a product is described. We do not sell links, we do not publish sponsored articles disguised as tutorials, and we do not accept compensation for coverage.

Where a guide includes affiliate links, the revenue arrangement is disclosed in the article itself and in our [Disclaimer](/disclaimer/).

## 2. How a guide is produced

**Research.** The writer starts from primary sources: official documentation, manufacturer instructions, standard-setting bodies (for electrical safety, food temperatures and the like), and hands-on testing on real devices and in real kitchens, workshops and gardens. Where a claim rests on a source, we link to it.

**Drafting.** The draft is written to a fixed structure: what you are trying to achieve, what you need before you start, the steps in order, what to do when it goes wrong, and the questions readers actually ask. Every guide includes:

- A clear tools-and-prerequisites section before step one
- Numbered steps that do not skip the awkward parts
- A troubleshooting section for the failures we know happen
- An FAQ section answering the related questions people search for

**Review.** A second editor who did not write the guide works through it against the live task. Steps that are ambiguous, out of order, or missing a warning go back to the writer. Safety-relevant content is checked against the relevant authority (manufacturer manual, government guidance, food-safety tables).

**Publication.** Only after review does a guide go live, with a date, an update date, a word count and a stated reading time.

**Maintenance.** Guides are re-checked on a rolling schedule and immediately when a reader reports that a step no longer works. The "Updated" date on each article tells you when that last happened.

## 3. What we guarantee about our content

- **Original** - every article is written by us, in our own words. We do not copy or lightly rewrite other people's work, and we do not buy pre-written "unique" articles.
- **Tested or verified** - we run the process ourselves where it is possible to do so; where a task cannot be replicated (for example, something tied to a specific device we do not own), we verify against primary documentation and say so.
- **Sourced** - claims that can be checked are linked to their source.
- **Dated and updated** - content carries publish and update dates, and material changes trigger an update note rather than a silent edit.
- **Corrected in public** - factual errors are fixed promptly; significant corrections are noted on the article rather than hidden.

## 4. What we will never publish

- Copied, spun or auto-generated filler written to manipulate search rankings
- Misleading headlines that do not match the article
- Fake "expert" authorship or invented testimonials
- Undisclosed paid placement, link sales or review compensation
- Content that encourages dangerous, illegal or harmful activity

## 5. Use of AI tools

We use modern tooling for research assistance, outlining, fact-checking support and SEO analysis - the same back-office help any modern publication uses. **A human editor is responsible for every sentence that reaches this site**: they draft, verify, rewrite and sign off. No article is published without human review against its sources, and no AI-generated text is published unedited. If our process for this ever changes, this section changes first, with a date.

## 6. Corrections policy

Found something wrong? Tell us via our [Contact page](/contact/) with the guide URL and what is incorrect.

- **Verified errors** - corrected as fast as we can, normally within 7 days, and the article's update date changes.
- **Substantive corrections** (a step that would have caused damage or a wrong safety figure) - fixed within 48 hours and the article carries a correction note.
- **Reader suggestions** that are opinion or a different-but-valid method - evaluated fairly; we may add them as an alternative approach, but we do not delete accurate content to settle a disagreement.

We keep a log of significant corrections internally and credit readers who spot errors when they want the credit.

## 7. Conflicts of interest

Editors must disclose any personal, financial or employment relationship with a product, service or brand they cover. Where a conflict exists, another editor reviews the piece. Free review samples are disclosed in the article and returned or donated where practical.

## 8. Sources and attribution

We quote sparingly and attribute clearly, link to primary sources rather than secondary summaries, and prefer official documentation over blog posts. Statistics are linked to the study or dataset they come from. If a source requires a paywall, we say so.

## 9. Content we decline

We decline requests to: remove accurate negative information in exchange for payment; add links for money; publish "review" articles whose conclusions are dictated in advance; or cover regulated professional advice (medical, legal, financial) beyond general informational guidance. See our [Disclaimer](/disclaimer/) for where our informational scope ends.

## 10. Contact

Editorial questions, corrections and complaints go through our [Contact page](/contact/). We answer editorial correspondence within 3 business days.
