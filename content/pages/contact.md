## Before you write

The fastest answer to most questions is already on the site. Each guide includes a **Frequently Asked Questions** section and a **table of contents**, and you can search all 80+ guides with the search icon in the menu.

If you cannot find what you need, we would genuinely like to hear from you.

## Email

*Email:* [makorig00@gmail.com](mailto:makorig00@gmail.com)

To help us reply usefully, please include:

- The **URL of the guide** you are writing about (if applicable)
- What you were trying to do and **where the steps failed**
- Your device, operating system or app version, when the issue is technical
- Whether you are reporting a **correction**, a **broken link**, or asking a **new question**

## What we answer, and how fast

| Type of message | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| Corrections and broken steps | Verified and fixed first; we aim to update the guide within 7 days |
| Questions about a guide | A reply from an editor, usually within 3 business days |
| New guide requests | Added to our editorial backlog and reviewed monthly |
| Privacy or data requests | Handled within 30 days as described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) |
| Partnership or advertising | Reply within 5 business days; we do not sell links or sponsored posts |

## What we cannot do

- We cannot provide one-to-one tech support, in-home repair or personal consulting.
- We cannot answer medical, legal or financial questions - please consult a qualified professional.
- We cannot guarantee a reply to every message, but every correction report is read.

## Editorial independence

Advertising partners and affiliate programs have no influence over what we publish or how we rate anything. To learn how guides are researched and updated, read our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/).

We do not accept payment for links, articles or rankings. Unsolicited promotional emails are deleted without a reply.
