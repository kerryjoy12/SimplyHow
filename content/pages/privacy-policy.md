This Privacy Policy explains how SimplyHow ("we", "our", "us") handles information. The site does not require an account. As currently configured, it does not run analytics or serve advertising.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information collected automatically

When you visit, the web host may process technical request data such as your IP address, browser, requested page, referring page, and request time to deliver and secure the site. The host's own privacy and retention practices apply to its server logs.

- **Cookies and local storage** - the site itself currently does not set cookies or store consent preferences. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/) for details.

### Information you give us voluntarily

- **Email messages** - if you contact us at the address on our [Contact page](/contact/), we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. Google processes email sent to our Gmail inbox under its own terms and privacy policy. We use messages to respond and retain them only as needed for that communication or a legal obligation.
- We do not currently provide a comments or account feature.

We do not ask for, and do not want, sensitive personal data. Please do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents by email.

## 2. How we use information

- To operate, maintain and secure the website
- To maintain the site and respond to email you send us
- To comply with legal obligations

We do **not** use your information to build profiles for sale, and we do not share it with third parties except as described below.

## 3. Cookies

The site currently has no analytics or advertising integrations and does not itself set cookies or access browser local storage.

Our hosting provider or third-party sites you choose to visit may use their own technologies under their own policies. See the [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).

## 4. Advertising

We do not currently serve ads or load Google AdSense code. If advertising is added, this policy will be updated before ads are served.

If Google advertising is enabled in the future, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this or other websites. Users can manage personalized advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) or learn about third-party opt-outs at [YourAdChoices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/). We will also provide any consent mechanism required for the locations and advertising features we serve.

Google's advertising technology and policies are described at [Google Advertising](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## 5. Third-party links and embedded content

Our guides link to external sites and may load images hosted by third parties. When your browser requests those resources, the provider may receive technical request data such as your IP address. These providers operate under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

## 6. Analytics

We do not currently use an analytics service. If one is added, we will update this policy to identify it and explain the information it collects.

## 7. Your rights

### GDPR (European Economic Area, UK)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectify** inaccurate data
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten") where we have no overriding reason to keep it
- **Restrict or object** to processing
- **Data portability**, where processing is automated and consent-based
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing that already took place

To make a privacy request, email us using the [Contact page](/contact/). Applicable rights and response timelines depend on the law that applies to your request.

### CCPA / CPRA (California residents)

If you are a California resident, applicable law may provide rights to know, access, delete, or opt out of certain uses of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email us using the [Contact page](/contact/).

### Do Not Track

Some browsers send a Do Not Track signal. There is no common industry standard for responding to it, so the site does not currently alter its behavior based on that signal.

## 8. Children's privacy

SimplyHow is written for a general adult audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

## 9. Data security

Use the contact details on the site carefully and do not email passwords, payment details, or identity documents. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure.

## 10. Data retention

The hosting provider controls its server-log retention. Email messages are retained only as needed for the communication or a legal obligation.

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the site or its practices change. The page header shows its publication/update date.

## 12. Contact

Questions about privacy or requests concerning your information: contact us using the details on our [Contact page](/contact/).
