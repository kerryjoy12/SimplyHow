This Privacy Policy explains what happens to information when you visit SimplyHow ("we", "our", "us"). We have written it in plain language because a policy nobody can read helps nobody. In short: we do not sell your data, we do not require an account, and the only personal information we collect is what your browser sends automatically and what you choose to give us.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information collected automatically

Like virtually every website, our servers automatically record information your browser sends:

- **Log data** - IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring page, pages visited, and the date and time of each request. Log data is used in aggregate to understand which guides are useful and to detect abuse.
- **Cookies and similar technologies** - small text files stored on your device. Section 3 and our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/) explain exactly which cookies we use and why.

### Information you give us voluntarily

- **Email messages** - if you contact us at the address on our [Contact page](/contact/), we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to answer you, and we keep it only as long as needed to resolve the request.
- **Corrections and comments** - if you report an error in a guide, we may record your name (if you give it) alongside the correction for our editorial records.

We do not ask for, and do not want, sensitive personal data. Please do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents by email.

## 2. How we use information

- To operate, maintain and secure the website
- To answer messages you send us
- To improve our guides (for example, spotting a step that confuses many readers)
- To understand traffic in aggregate
- To display advertising and measure its performance
- To comply with legal obligations

We do **not** use your information to build profiles for sale, and we do not share it with third parties except as described below.

## 3. Cookies

Cookies fall into three groups on this site:

1. **Strictly necessary** - remember your cookie consent choice (`simplyhow_cookie_consent`). Without it the banner would reappear on every page.
2. **Analytics** - help us understand which guides are read and where readers drop off, recorded in aggregate.
3. **Advertising** - used by our advertising partner to serve and measure ads. See section 4.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; blocking the consent cookie only means the banner shows again. Full detail is in our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).

## 4. Google AdSense and advertising cookies

We use **Google AdSense** to display advertising. AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve advertisements to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting **Google Ads Settings** (<https://www.google.com/settings/ads>). Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at **About Ads** (<https://optout.aboutads.info/>).

Google's DoubleClick advertising cookie may also be used to show you relevant ads on other websites based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

Specifically:

- Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.
- Google's use of the DoubleClick cookie enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You may opt out of the DoubleClick cookie for personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads) or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at [optout.aboutads.info](https://optout.aboutads.info/).
- You can also review Google's own policies at <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>.

Our advertising partners may include Google and other ad networks. We do not control the cookies they set beyond choosing to work with reputable networks that comply with industry standards.

## 5. Third-party links and embedded content

Our guides link to external sites - manufacturer documentation, government resources, product pages - and may occasionally embed content (for example, a YouTube tutorial). Those sites collect data under **their own** privacy policies, which we do not control. We encourage you to read the policy of any external site you visit.

## 6. Analytics

We use privacy-conscious, aggregate analytics to count visits and pageviews. Analytics data shows us things like which guides are popular and what search terms brought readers in. It does not identify you personally and we do not combine it with data from other sites.

## 7. Your rights

### GDPR (European Economic Area, UK)

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Rectify** inaccurate data
- **Erase** your data ("right to be forgotten") where we have no overriding reason to keep it
- **Restrict or object** to processing
- **Data portability**, where processing is automated and consent-based
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing that already took place

To exercise any of these rights, email us. We respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

### CCPA / CPRA (California residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of that information, and request that it not be sold. **We do not sell personal information** in the sense the CCPA defines it, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising beyond the standard advertising cookies described in section 4. To submit a request, email us; we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

### Do Not Track

Some browsers send a Do Not Track signal. There is no common industry standard for responding to it, so we do not change our behavior based on DNT headers. You can achieve the same effect by blocking advertising cookies as described above.

## 8. Children's privacy

SimplyHow is written for a general adult audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

## 9. Data security

We transmit this site over HTTPS and keep administrative access limited and protected by strong authentication. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we hold.

## 10. Data retention

Server logs are retained only as long as needed for security and aggregate analysis. Emails are kept until the matter is resolved and then deleted or archived. Cookie lifetimes are described in the [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/).

## 11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the law, our practices or our tools. The update date at the top of this page changes whenever we do. Material changes will be highlighted on the homepage for at least 14 days.

## 12. Contact

Questions about privacy, or requests to access or delete your data: see our [Contact page](/contact/) for the current email address. We answer every legitimate privacy request within 30 days.
